## AP2: Multi-Rule Temporal Interaction Threats
Adversarial outcomes arising from temporal interaction of multiple individually-correct rules, including:
- Cascade chaining (A triggers B triggers C)
- Timer/state override (B preempts A's in-flight action)
- Resource contention (concurrent A and B target same resource)
- Interleaving (events arrive between A's trigger and A's action — overlap with AP6 TOCTOU)
